One desk · two planes

Intelligence can be probabilistic.
Authority cannot.

Mandate separates the software that seeks returns from the software that controls capital. The desk may form opinions; the control plane must produce the same decision from the same facts every time.

01Intelligence plane

Find and construct opportunity.

Research agents and deterministic models assemble the investable universe, score assets, propose allocations, and watch the book between cycles.

  • Asset sourcing and eligibility signals
  • Research, scoring, and thesis formation
  • Portfolio construction and drift monitoring
Produces proposals—not execution authority.
02Authority plane

Decide what may touch capital.

A deterministic engine evaluates the entire portfolio, binds approval to the exact order, and gives the credentialed relay only the authority it needs.

  • Cross-strategy exposure and liquidity limits
  • Single-use, order-bound execution permits
  • Revocation, circuit breakers, and receipts
Holds the boundary every strategy must clear.

The system

Three pieces. One hard boundary.

Mandate is not a cast of agents. It is a portfolio engine connected to a small authorization kernel and a relay that possesses exactly one dangerous capability: execution.

01
PORTFOLIO ENGINE

Turn market intelligence into proposals.

Sources eligible assets, forms views, constructs the target book, and submits order intents. Models can change without gaining execution authority.

Output · portfolio intent
02
MANDATE KERNEL

Compute the authority of every action.

Combines policy, current holdings, eligibility, liquidity, valuation freshness, and cross-strategy exposure into one reproducible decision.

Output · permit or denial
03
EXECUTION RELAY

Translate narrow permission into action.

Holds the venue credential, validates the permit, submits the exact approved order, and returns the fill to portfolio state.

Output · fill receipt
fills + receipts→portfolio state updates→the next proposal sees reality

The technical primitive

No permit.
No route.

Agents never receive the venue credential. They submit intents to Mandate. The policy engine either returns a denial receipt or creates a short-lived permit bound to the exact asset, size, price bounds, account, and destination.

01

One execution pathThe credentialed relay is the only component allowed to reach the venue.

02

Order-bound authorityChange the amount, asset, price, account, or route and the permit becomes invalid.

03

Inspectable proofEvery approval, denial, fill, and revocation becomes a replayable receipt.

Asset coverage

One book. Different proofs.

Every asset class needs its own eligibility and settlement evidence. Mandate should share one authorization model without pretending the underlying assets are interchangeable.

Tokenized equities

Canonical contract, jurisdiction, market status, price freshness, account eligibility, and venue support.

Registry adapter planned

Private assets

Issuer authority, investor eligibility, transfer restrictions, valuation age, custody, and exit liquidity.

Policy research

Real-world assets

Issuer, reserves, redemption terms, custody, maturity, settlement route, and independent attestations.

Policy research

Robinhood Chain and Robinhood MCP are candidate integrations, not part of this prototype. Mandate is independent and not affiliated with Robinhood. Availability and eligibility vary by jurisdiction.
